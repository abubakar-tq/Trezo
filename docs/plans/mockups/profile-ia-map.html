The 8 cuts (how ~31 → ~15):
DELETE ×3ConnectedDevices (hardcoded mock, all buttons no-op) · Settings legacy (disconnected duplicate of theme+signout+notifs) · SecurityPrivacy (pure passthrough to 2 screens already reachable in 1 tap). Zero capability lost.
GATE ×3Move behind __DEV__, out of the production nav: Email Recovery Start, the Guardian timelock picker + raw UserOp payload dumps, Check Passkey Authority debug. (Dev Controls already dev-gated.)
MERGE ×2Recovery Attempt Status + Group Status → one status screen (group = a section/accordion, not a 4th hop). Email Recovery monolith (2263 lines) → split into a setup wizard vs a management view (kills shipped-but-hidden dead UI paths).
REGROUPRoot reorganized into Security / Wallet / Preferences (was Account / Preferences). Recovery becomes one clear hub; the 9 recovery-feature screens stay reachable but flatten from 4+ taps to ≤2 under that hub.
